NFT Staking

Staking controls for eligible ERC-721 collections.

Connect wallet and configure the staking contract. Transactions require the exact deployed ABI and must be tested against that deployment.

Collection Registry

Register an NFT collection and review its status. Registration uses the protocol's configured CATA fees.

Registry actions require the exact deployed ABI and verified collection-qualification flow.

Governance

Proposal creation, voting, status checks and execution.

Governance writes must use the final verifier ABI, proof format and target/selector restrictions. Do not submit proposals until those checks are confirmed.

Treasury

Treasury controls are governance-gated. This panel will display or invoke only methods present in the finalized treasury ABI.

Treasury disbursements must be authorized by the approved governance flow and satisfy the spend limits.

Guardian Security

Recovery and guardian actions must follow the configured guardian quorum, delays and allowlist.

Guardian methods and role checks must match the finalized guardian ABI. Do not use recovery controls without verifying the deployed implementation.

CATA Token

Read your balance on the currently connected network.

— CATA
The token balance reader uses the standard ERC-20 balanceOf and decimals methods. Other token operations require the token ABI and role verification.

Network contract addresses

Enter actual deployed addresses separately for each network. Settings stay in this browser and are not sent to a server.

Do not enter guessed addresses. Use addresses from verified deployments on the selected network.

Remix VM deployment reference

These addresses were supplied from your Remix VM deployment. They are saved here only as a reference and are deliberately not assigned to Polygon Amoy or Polygon PoS, because Remix VM addresses are not Polygon deployments.

Do not paste these into the Amoy or mainnet address settings. Deploy each finalized contract to the target network first, then enter the new addresses in that network's configuration.

Connection & safety

This file never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Wallet signatures and transactions are handled by your wallet.
